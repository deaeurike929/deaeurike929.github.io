Research & concept

Venue research, content ideas, and a pitch to the team lead before anything is filmed.

2

Hook & script

A hook for the first seconds, plus the voice-over script and copy.

3

Film

On-location videography and photography.

4

Edit

Short-form edit with motion graphics, text animation, color, and sound design.

5

Captions & delivery

Subtitles and captions, then delivery ready for Instagram and TikTok.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Short-form video for brands and events: product videos, venue and food features, barber and grooming content, and event coverage. I edit mainly in Premiere Pro, After Effects, and CapCut.

How involved are you in the process?

Fully hands-on. I handle concept, scripting, filming, and the edit with motion graphics and text animation, plus voice-over and captions. At BDGFOLK I deliver venue features end to end, and at BAD LAB I own the workflow from ideas and hooks to the final edit.

Do you take small projects, or only big ones?

Both. I've done 3–5 freelance projects a month since 2023, like birthday videos and Sweet Seventeen events, and I currently produce 60 short-form videos a month for BAD LAB's two accounts.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the number of videos and how much filming is involved. Tell me your deadline and I'll say honestly what's realistic.

Do you work alone or with a team?

I work independently from idea to final file, and I'm comfortable pitching to and working with a team lead or creative lead.

Can you match my brand's style?

Yes. I produce for two very different brands at once: a men's grooming brand and a food and lifestyle media platform, each with its own voice.
