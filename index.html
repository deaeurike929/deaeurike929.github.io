How a video gets made.

The same five steps on every project, whether it's one Reel or sixty videos a month.

1

Research & concept

I start with your brief and research the location or subject. Then I develop content ideas and pitch the concept for approval before anything is filmed.

2

Hook & script

I write the hook for the opening seconds, then the voice-over script and on-screen copy.

3

Film

I film on location, with photography when the project calls for it. If you only need editing, you can send your own footage.

4

Edit

I cut for short-form: pacing, zooms, transitions, and effects, with motion graphics and text animation where they fit. Then I finish with color grading, music, and sound design.

5

Captions & delivery

I add subtitles and captions, then export for Instagram and TikTok. Final videos usually run from 30 seconds to 5 minutes.

Your questions answered.

Something missing? Send me a message and I'll reply directly.

What kind of projects do you take on?

I take on a wide mix of projects: events, brand content, collaborations, and more, shaped around what each client needs. My focus is short-form content, with final videos usually running from 30 seconds to 5 minutes.

How involved are you in the process?

Fully hands-on, from the first concept and script, through filming, to the final edit, color grading, and sound design. I can also take on just the part you need, whether that’s editing only, videography only, or something else.

Do you take small projects, or only big ones?

I’m open to projects of every size, as long as they align with my creative vision and the idea resonates with me. Whether it’s a single short-form edit or a full production, I’m happy to talk.

How long does a project take?

Simple short-form edits can take as little as one day, with a capacity of 3–5 videos per day depending on the footage and editing requirements. More complex edits take longer, and full-production projects typically take 3–7 days. Timelines are tailored to each project’s scope and creative needs.

Do you work alone or with a team?

I handle every project myself, from concept to final edit. For larger projects, I’m used to collaborating with a few trusted people, so the final result and the creative vision get the attention they need.

Can you match my brand's style?

Yes. I adapt to what the project needs, whether that means matching your existing visual style or building a new creative direction with you.

Are you available for remote or full-time work?

Yes. I’m available for project-based, full-time, freelance, and remote work. I’m based in Jakarta, Indonesia.

How does pricing work?

Pricing is project-based and depends on the number of videos, their complexity, and their final duration. Contact me directly with your project details and I’ll send you a clear quote.
